COOKIE
POLICY.

LAST UPDATED: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

What changed. The landing page still sets no cookies. The game, however, now writes to your browser's local storage in order to remember your Guardian callsign — that is a storage operation on your device, and it belongs in this policy even though it is not technically a cookie.

1. Cookies and equivalent storage

European rules on storing information on a user's device — Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, transposed in Italy at Art. 122 of the Data Protection Code — apply to cookies and to any equivalent technique, including localStorage. What matters is not the technology but whether the storage is strictly necessary for a service the user has expressly requested.

2. What this landing page uses

Nothing. This page sets no cookies of its own, embeds no analytics, no advertising pixel, no video player, no map and no remote fonts. Typefaces are resolved from those already installed on your device, which is why no request leaves for a font provider.

For this reason no consent banner is shown on this page.

3. What the game stores on your device

KeyContentsPurposeDuration
scudo.identityYour Guardian callsign and a random identifier generated on first visitKeeps your identity between runs so scores stay attached to youUntil you clear browser storage
scudo.dbYour local scores and profileOnly used when no online leaderboard is configured, so the game works offlineUntil you clear browser storage

Both entries exist to deliver a feature you request explicitly, by typing a callsign and pressing Play. Neither is used for profiling, advertising or measurement, and neither is read by any third party. No expiry is set by the browser: they persist until you remove them.

4. How to remove them

Clearing site data in your browser settings deletes both entries and, with them, your local identity. In Chrome and Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies and site data. In Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data. In Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data. Private or incognito browsing discards them when the window closes.

Removing local storage does not erase scores already submitted to an online leaderboard: for those, see the rights section of the Privacy Policy.

5. External services

The Play links open the game, hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening any external domain may involve technical processing by that provider under its own policies. Where the online leaderboard is enabled, the game also contacts the database provider named in the Privacy Policy in order to submit and read scores; that connection is initiated only after you have entered a callsign and finished a run.

6. If tracking is ever added

Should analytics, advertising, embedded video or any other non-essential technique be introduced later, this page must be updated and a compliant consent mechanism — with the ability to refuse and to withdraw — must be in place before those tools are loaded, not after.

7. References

Regulation (EU) 2016/679; Art. 122 of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 as amended; the Garante's guidelines on cookies and other tracking tools of 10 June 2021; and the European Data Protection Board's guidance on consent and on the technical scope of Art. 5(3) ePrivacy.
