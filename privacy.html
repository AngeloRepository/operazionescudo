2. Two possible configurations

The game ships in one of two states, and which one applies changes this policy substantially:

  • Online leaderboard. A backend is configured. The data listed in section 3 is transmitted to and stored on that server so that scores can be ranked against other players.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Storing the callsign, identifier and scores serves to deliver the competitive feature you asked for by entering a callsign and finishing a run: the legal basis is performance of the service requested by you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), or your consent where that is the more appropriate basis in your jurisdiction.

Anti-cheat validation, rate limiting and the anomaly log rest on the controller's legitimate interest in keeping the leaderboard meaningful and the service available (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Technical logs kept by hosting providers rest on the same basis, together with Art. 32 GDPR on security of processing.

7. Your rights

Under Arts. 15–22 GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw any consent given, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Because the leaderboard identity is a callsign plus a random identifier, please include your callsign in any request so the record can be located. Requests go to [PRIVACY EMAIL]. You may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority; in Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children and asks for no data beyond a chosen callsign. If you believe a child has entered identifying information as a callsign, write to [PRIVACY EMAIL] and it will be removed.

9. Security

Where the online leaderboard is active, the client cannot write to the database directly: all writes pass through validated server-side functions, and row-level security denies direct insertion and modification. Appropriate technical and organisational measures are applied in proportion to the risk, under Art. 32 GDPR.

10. Changes

This policy will be updated if the site, the game or the services behind them change. The published version always carries the date of its last update.
